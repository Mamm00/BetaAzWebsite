About Al Azhar Tex

A family fabric house in Zagazig, with a real gallery that keeps its door open to every buyer.

Al Azhar Tex is a family fabric house in Zagazig, Sharkia governorate. The gallery sits in the Ziraa district past the Abu Hammad car station — a location every local knows: directly opposite the station.

Our stock covers every category, with women's apparel fabrics as the core line: chiffon, crepe, satin, viscose, cotton voile, jersey, single knit, lace, tulle, organza, brocade, linen blends, linings and more. Every line is stocked in three quality grades: A, B and C.

Our philosophy is plain: write the grade on the bolt, put it under the light, and let you see and decide for yourself. Nothing is hidden from the buyer.
