Why we grade openly

Sellers who hide the grade are not hurting the fabric — they are hurting trust. We write the grade on every bolt, put it under the light, and let you see it yourself. Grade C is no shame: it is clearance in bulk at a price that earns its place, and buyers who know what they are doing choose it on purpose.

— Shady Anwar
